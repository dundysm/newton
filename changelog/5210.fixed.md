Warn when USD import drops authored nonzero `physxRigidBody:linearDamping` or `physxRigidBody:angularDamping` on an imported rigid body, naming the prim and attribute. An authored finite 0 and unauthored attributes are not reported. A body custom attribute that reads the USD attribute, such as `SolverSteel.register_custom_attributes()`, imports the value and suppresses the warning. The warning does not apply the damping to `SolverMuJoCo` or any other solver.
